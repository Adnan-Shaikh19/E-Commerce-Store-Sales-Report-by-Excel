# 📊 E-Commerce Store Annual Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project provides an end-to-end analysis of an e-commerce store's annual sales performance (over 31,000 orders and ₹2.1M in sales revenue).
Using **Microsoft Excel**, raw sales transactional data was cleaned, transformed, and structured into an interactive executive dashboard to evaluate customer demographics, regional contribution, sales channels, and order fulfillment trends.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- **Sales Trend Analysis:** Compare monthly order counts against gross revenue.
- **Demographic Breakdown:** Analyze customer purchasing behavior by age and gender.
- **Channel Performance:** Determine revenue share across platforms (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, etc.).
- **Regional Analysis:** Identify top revenue-generating states.
- **Fulfillment Tracking:** Monitor order statuses (Delivered, Cancelled, Returned, Refunded).

---

## 🛠️ Key Data Operations & Tooling
- **Tool:** Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Formatting)
- **Data Cleansing:** Standardized inconsistent category fields, converted dates to monthly periods, and mapped customer ages into demographic bins (Teenager, Adult, Senior).
- **Interactive UI:** Configured dynamic slicers for **Month**, **Category**, and **Channel** to enable deep-dive filtering across charts.

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Business Findings
1. **Target Demographic:** **Women drive 64%** of total revenue, with Adult Females (~34.6%) and Teenage Females (~21.1%) forming the highest-converting customer segments.
2. **Top Sales Channels:** **Amazon (~35.5%)**, **Flipkart (~23.3%)**, and **Myntra (~21.6%)** account for over **80%** of total distribution.
3. **Geographic Hotspots:** Sales are heavily concentrated in **Maharashtra** (₹2.98M) and **Karnataka** (₹2.64M).
4. **Order Fulfillment:** Maintained a strong **92% delivery success rate**, with combined cancellations and returns under **8%**.
