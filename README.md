# E-Commerce-Store-Sales-Report-by-Excel

Project Overview

  This project focuses on analyzing an annual sales dataset for an e-commerce business to evaluate performance, understand customer demographic patterns, and       evaluate sales channel efficiency. By processing raw operational data and building an interactive dashboard in Excel, key insights were derived to support        business strategy for driving future revenue growth.

Business Objective

  The primary goal is to generate actionable insights into customer purchasing behavior across regions, demographics, and channels to help the store optimize       marketing efforts and increase sales. Key business questions addressed include:
  Comparing overall order volumes against total sales revenue over time.
  Identifying peak sales months and demand trends.   
  Analyzing buyer demographics across age groups and gender combinations.
  Evaluating channel-wise revenue distribution (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, etc.).
  Tracking overall order fulfillment statuses (Delivered, Cancelled, Returned, Refunded).
  Identifying top-performing geographic states by revenue generation.

Data Processing & CleaningData Cleansing:

  Standardized missing/inconsistent entries across categories, order statuses, and sales channels.
  Data Formatting: Applied proper currency formatting for revenue fields, categorized age metrics into distinct demographics (Adult, Senior, Teenager), and         extracted monthly periods for seasonal comparison.

Data Aggregation: 
  
  Built Pivot Tables to summarize high-volume transactions across months, channels, regions, and demographics.
  Visualizations & Dashboard FeaturesKey Performance Indicators (KPIs): Instant display of Total Revenue (₹2,117,637) and Total Orders (31,047).
  Order vs. Sales Trend: A dual-axis combo chart illustrating sales values and order counts across all 12 months.   Demographic Breakdown:Gender Ratio:             Visualizing purchasing contributions between Men (36%) and Women (64%).

Age & Gender Matrix:
  
  
  Segmenting purchases across Adult (34.59% Women, 15.47% Men), Teenager (21.13% Women, 9.20% Men), and Senior groups.
  Top Revenue-Generating States: Horizontal bar graph ranking top states (led by Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh).
  Channel Distribution: Pie chart detailing market share across primary platforms 
  (Amazon leading at ~35.45%, followed by Flipkart at ~23.31% and Myntra at ~21.59%).

Order Fulfillment Tracking: Distribution chart showcasing delivery performance (92% Delivered, 3% Cancelled, 3% Returned, 2% Refunded).

Interactive Slicers: Dynamic filters for Month, Category, and Channel to enable custom detailed analysis.

Key Business Findings & InsightsTarget Audience Profile:
  Women (64%) are the primary buyers, with Adult Females (~34.6%) and Teenager Females (~21.1%) representing the largest purchasing demographics.

Top Performing Regions: Sales are strongly concentrated in South and West India, with Maharashtra (₹2.98M) and Karnataka (₹2.64M) driving the highest revenue.   

Primary Sales Channels: Amazon (~35.5%), Flipkart (~23.3%), and Myntra (~21.6%) combined make up over 80% of total order distribution. 

Fulfillment Performance: High delivery success rate (92%), with returns and cancellations restricted to 8% in total.

Recommendations to Drive Future GrowthTargeted Ad Campaigns: Concentrate marketing efforts and promotions toward adult and teenage female buyers.

Channel Optimization: Focus inventory allocation and ad spend on top-performing platforms (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra) while exploring targeted strategies for lower-volume channels like Ajio and Nalli.   

Regional Campaigns: Prioritize state-level targeted marketing and localized stocking in high-demand regions such as Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.
